Use of this configuration

A personal integration for the configuration owner’s own mail accounts.

This OAuth configuration connects the owner’s Google accounts to the Nail macOS app. It is not offered as a public service, and this website does not offer accounts, subscriptions, or hosted email processing.

Use of Google services remains subject to Google’s applicable terms and account controls. Use of Nail remains subject to any terms provided by its developer. This page does not grant rights to Nail, represent its developer, or alter those terms.

The owner controls authorization, message actions, and local mail storage. Access can be revoked through Google Account connections.

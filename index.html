Personal OAuth configuration

Nail

A private Google mail connection for a macOS email client.

This page documents Matías’s personal Google OAuth configuration for Nail. It is used to connect the owner’s own Gmail and Google Workspace mail accounts to the Nail app on a Mac.

What the connection does

Nail uses Google sign-in to access the selected account’s email address and mail. Gmail access allows the app to read, compose, send, and organize messages, including deleting messages, as part of normal email-client use.

Google handles account sign-in. Nail stores the resulting sign-in tokens in macOS Keychain and downloaded mail locally on the Mac. This website does not receive Google sign-in tokens or mailbox contents.

About this configuration

This is a personal integration, not a public hosted email service. This website describes the owner’s configuration; it is not the Nail developer’s official website or a policy statement on the developer’s behalf.

Privacy policy

For the owner’s personal Google OAuth configuration used with Nail.

Scope

This policy describes the private Google OAuth configuration operated by Matías for connecting his own accounts to Nail on his Mac. It does not describe or make commitments on behalf of the Nail developer or replace Google’s privacy policy.

Google data and its use

The connection requests OpenID sign-in, the selected account’s email address, and the Gmail mail scope (https://mail.google.com/). Gmail permission allows reading, composing, sending, organizing, and permanently deleting mail. These permissions support the email-client functionality used by the account owner.

This personal configuration is used for the owner’s email tasks. The configuration owner does not operate a service that collects other people’s Google account data and does not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Storage and transfer

Google performs authentication. Nail stores Google sign-in tokens in macOS Keychain and downloaded messages in its local mail storage on the Mac. Mail connections communicate with Google’s mail servers over TLS. Sending mail transmits the chosen message to Google and the recipients selected by the account owner.

This static information website receives no OAuth callback, Google tokens, or mailbox contents. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process ordinary website request information under GitHub’s own privacy policy.

Control and retention

The owner can disconnect an account in Nail and revoke its Google access in Google Account connections. Revoking permission stops future authorized access; it does not automatically delete mail already downloaded to the Mac. The owner controls removal of local mail files and saved account data.

Google API policy

The configuration owner’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

The configuration owner is Matías (@matixlol). This policy applies only to this personal configuration.
